# Kya Baat Facts: content plan

Instagram: @kyabaatfacts (registered) · YouTube: @kyabaatfacts (registered) · Tagline: "Har fact pe: kya baat!"
Hindi/Hinglish narration, English on screen. Reels 60-120 s (9:16). Long videos 10-12 min (16:9).

## How this plan works
- **Reels R001-R146:** every title in posting order. Type: R = reveal, M = myth buster (the app's reel kinds). From R015 the order is a **fair rotation**
  (owner, 27 Nov 2026): each "Turn" (in Notes) gives every one of the 20 categories one reel before any category gets a second, so new categories get the same chance as old ones.
- **Long videos L01-L10:** one a month. Each gives 4-6 reels cut from it ("derived reels"), which count in the next 400.
- **Roadmap (the next ~400):** a roadmap of series and counts. Exact titles get written in batches of 50, using what the analytics show works.
- **Fact check:** every fact is checked against a real source before recording. The "note" column marks claims that are
  disputed or legendary; those are framed honestly ("kya sach mein?", "it's said..."). If a fact fails the check, it's swapped out.
- **Launch tip:** make R001-R010 before going public, so there's a 10-reel buffer.

## Fair rotation (replaces the old weekly rhythm)
Every category gets a fair chance: no fixed weekdays. The plan order cycles through all 20 categories (one reel each per "Turn"), and
Autopilot does the same when it fills the posting days: each day goes to the CHECKED reel whose category has waited longest since its last post.
Categories (channel.json → categories): Science Wala Sach · Itihaas Ke Raaz · Jaanwaron Ki Duniya · Antriksh · Desh Ke Ajoobe · Myth or Fact? ·
Khaane Ka Itihaas · Paise Ki Kahani · Shabdon Ki Kahani, and from 27 Nov 2026: **Agar Aisa Ho Toh?** (what if) · **Aisa Kyun?** (everyday why) ·
**Sharir Ka Sach** (your body) · **Tech Ka Jaadu** (how tech works) · **Duniya Ke Record** (extremes) · **Kisne Banaya?** (inventions) ·
**Ajeeb Duniya** (strange countries) · **Dimaag Ka Khel** (brain and mind) · **Samundar Ke Raaz** (oceans) · **Numbers Ka Jaadu** (maths) · **Bharat Ka Pehla** (India's firsts).
"What if" reels answer only from published science (NASA, physicists, medical sources) and say where the answer comes from; no guesswork.
When a category runs out of ideas, write 3-5 more for it in the next batch rather than letting it drop out.

## Production notes
- **Voice budget (ElevenLabs):** a reel needs about 1,000-1,500 narration characters, and a few takes each. 24 reels plus 1 long video
  a month is far more than the 40k-character plan the studio assumes, so pick a bigger plan or start at 3-4 reels a week.
- **Sessions:** the studio makes 3-4 reels per Claude session, so about 6-8 sessions a month at full pace.
- **Review at 48 h:** check where viewers drop off and move strong categories up in the next batch of 50.
