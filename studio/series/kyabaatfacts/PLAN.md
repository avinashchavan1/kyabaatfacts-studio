# Kya Baat Facts: content plan

Instagram: @kyabaatfacts (registered) · YouTube: @kyabaatfacts (registered) · Tagline: "Har fact pe: kya baat!"
Hindi/Hinglish narration, English on screen. Reels 60-120 s (9:16). Long videos 10-12 min (16:9).

## How this plan works
- **Reels R001-R100:** every title in posting order, 6 a week (Mon-Sat), about 17 weeks. Type: R = reveal, M = myth buster (the app's reel kinds); week and day are in Notes.
- **Long videos L01-L10:** one a month. Each gives 4-6 reels cut from it ("derived reels"), which count in the next 400.
- **Reels R101-R500:** a roadmap of series and counts. Exact titles get written in batches of 50, using what the analytics show works.
- **Fact check:** every fact is checked against a real source before recording. The "note" column marks claims that are
  disputed or legendary; those are framed honestly ("kya sach mein?", "it's said..."). If a fact fails the check, it's swapped out.
- **Launch tip:** make R001-R010 before going public, so there's a 10-reel buffer.

## Production notes
- **Voice budget (ElevenLabs):** a reel needs about 1,000-1,500 narration characters, and a few takes each. 24 reels plus 1 long video
  a month is far more than the 40k-character plan the studio assumes, so pick a bigger plan or start at 3-4 reels a week.
- **Sessions:** the studio makes 3-4 reels per Claude session, so about 6-8 sessions a month at full pace.
- **Review at 48 h:** check where viewers drop off and move strong categories up in the next batch of 50.
