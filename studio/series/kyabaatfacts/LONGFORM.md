# Kya Baat Facts: long-form plan

Owner, 27 Nov 2026: "I need long form type videos." One full-length video a month (10-12 min, 16:9, YouTube), each a NEW production
whose beats are drawn so 4-6 reels can be cut from them (PLAYBOOK 22-28). This file is the plan; each video's working file is
`longform/<code>/longform.json` (synced to the app's Long videos page).

## Why long videos
- **Monetisation:** YouTube Partner Program counts long-video watch hours (4,000 h in 12 months) or Shorts views (10 M in 90 days).
  One 11-minute video watched halfway by 1,000 people is ~90 watch hours; Shorts alone take far longer to qualify.
- **Depth and trust:** a fact channel that goes deep (sources on screen, chapters) gets subscribed to, not just swiped past.
- **Cheaper reels:** every long video yields 4-6 finished reels from the same research, voice and art.

## Format (every long video)
| Part | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 0:00-0:30 | The single most surprising claim of the video, shown, not told (best proof photo + motion kit). No logo intro. |
| Promise | 0:30-0:50 | "Aaj 10 jagah, aur number 1 pe tum yakeen nahi karoge" style: tells the viewer what they get and why to stay to the end. |
| Beats | 8-10 × 60-75 s | Each beat is self-contained: claim → proof (2-3 real photos, map, numbers) → "kya baat hai" reveal. Chapter card at the start. |
| Re-hooks | every ~2.5 min | A one-line tease of what's coming ("aur aage wala toh... physics ko chunauti deta hai"). |
| Payoff + loop | last 60 s | The strongest beat last (countdown order), a one-line recap, SUBSCRIBE, end screen to the next long video. |

- Narrator Bunty (same voice and energy as the reels), English subtitles, sources on screen when a claim surprises, all claims listed with
  publisher + URL in the description, YouTube chapters from the beat cards.
- Art: 1920×1080 with every beat's action inside the centred 9:16 safe zone (one drawing, both frames), motion kit on
  (`KB_MOTION`), 4-8 real photos per video (maps and ground photos especially), music per act (3-4 pieces, crossfaded).
- Thumbnail: 16:9, one huge object + 3-4 words + the googly eyes; never a misleading claim.
- Budget: ~15-20k ElevenLabs characters per video (two takes). Plan the month's reels around it.

## Posting
- Autopilot posts an APPROVED long video to YouTube on **Sunday at 11:00 IST** (the channel's best YouTube slot), at most one a week,
  without taking that day's reel slot. Review it in the app like a reel (tick the citation check), and Autopilot does the rest.
- Derived reels go into the plan's fair rotation like any reel, each ending with "poori video channel pe" (YouTube) in the caption.
