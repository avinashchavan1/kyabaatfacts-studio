## Framing
- Say "lagbhag 50 saal" (49 years). Say "hazaaron kitaabein" (library size figures disagree), never an exact number.
- Drop: dyslexia (speculation, little evidence per Scroll.in), the "Navratna" legend, "so he could understand it" as the stated purpose of the Razmnama.
- Akbar is drawn without a face (turban, book, pen, scroll): no invented likeness of a real person.
