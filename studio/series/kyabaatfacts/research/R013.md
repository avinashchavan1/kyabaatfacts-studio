# R013 · Bachche 300 haddiyon ke saath, bade hokar 206 kyun? (Science Wala Sach)

Researched 25 Nov 2026 by web search (result text read, not memory).

| # | Claim | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | A newborn's skeleton has about 270-300 bones (some still partly cartilage); an adult has 206 | https://health.clevelandclinic.org/how-many-bones-does-a-baby-have ; https://www.britannica.com/one-good-fact/what-do-babies-have-more-of-than-adults ; https://www.mentalfloss.com/posts/how-many-bones-in-human-body |
| C2 | The number drops because many small bones fuse into bigger ones as we grow | https://health.clevelandclinic.org/how-many-bones-does-a-baby-have ; https://www.britannica.com/one-good-fact/what-do-babies-have-more-of-than-adults |
| C3 | The baby's skull plates have soft spots (fontanelles) between them; the front one closes by about 1-2 years | https://health.clevelandclinic.org/fontanelle-baby-soft-spot ; https://www.upmc.com/healthbeat/pediatrics/baby-fontanelles |
| C4 | The sacrum starts as 5 separate vertebrae that fuse into one bone, finishing in the late teens to mid-20s | https://www.britannica.com/science/sacrum ; https://bio.libretexts.org/Courses/Lemoore_College/Human_Anatomy_Laboratory_Manual_(Hartline)/08%3A_Axial_Skeleton/8.05%3A_Vertebral_Column/8.5.06%3A_Sacrum_and_Coccyx |
| C5 | The tailbone (coccyx) is 3-5 small vertebrae fused together | https://bio.libretexts.org/Courses/Lemoore_College/Human_Anatomy_Laboratory_Manual_(Hartline)/08%3A_Axial_Skeleton/8.05%3A_Vertebral_Column/8.5.06%3A_Sacrum_and_Coccyx ; https://www.elsevier.com/resources/anatomy/skeletal-system/axial-skeleton/coccyx/21141 |

Framing: "lagbhag 300" (estimates 270-300; Cleveland Clinic 275-300). Avoid an exact 300; avoid "everyone has exactly 206" (some people have extra/unfused bones).
