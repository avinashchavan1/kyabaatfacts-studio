# R011 · Great Wall space se dikhti hai? MYTH! (Myth or Fact?)

Researched 25 Nov 2026 by web search (result text read, not memory) + NASA Image Library caption read directly (images-api.nasa.gov).

| # | Claim | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | The popular claim: the Great Wall is visible from space (even "from the Moon"); it was taught in textbooks | https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/is-chinas-great-wall-visible-from-space/ ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2003/10/17/chinas-spaceman-shatters-a-myth |
| C2 | NASA: the wall generally isn't visible to the unaided eye from low Earth orbit, and certainly not from the Moon | https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/PDFs/great_wall_less_great_from_space.pdf |
| C3 | The wall is only about 6 metres wide and blends with the colours of the land | https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2003/10/17/chinas-spaceman-shatters-a-myth ; https://www.britannica.com/question/Can-you-see-the-Great-Wall-of-China-from-space |
| C4 | China's first astronaut Yang Liwei (Shenzhou 5, Oct 2003) said on CCTV he did not see the Great Wall | https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2003/10/17/chinas-spaceman-shatters-a-myth ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenzhou_5 |
| C5 | ISS photo ISS010-E-08497 (24 Nov 2004, Leroy Chiao), "apparently the first verifiable photo" of the wall from low Earth orbit, taken with a 400 mm lens; only a small section is visible | https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss010e08497 |
| C6 | Many other human-made things are visible from low orbit, such as cities and roads | https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/PDFs/great_wall_less_great_from_space.pdf ; https://www.livescience.com/human-made-structures-seen-from-space |

Avoid: "never visible under any condition" (Britannica: sometimes visible from low orbit in the right light); the wall's total length (several figures); "Kevin" Hadfield (search snippet misnamed Chris Hadfield; not used).
