Framing: the 6→4 story is Shepherd-Barron's own account ("उन्होंने खुद बताया"). Avoid: "every PIN today is 4 digits" (many banks allow 4-6+), the "136,000 cheques" radiation claim (one source), who "really" invented the ATM (other inventors claim credit).
