Avoid: exact phosphorus % (sources differ: 0.25% vs ~1%); weight (sources differ: 3 vs 6 tonnes); who moved it to Delhi
(Iltutmish vs Anangpal, disputed); "never rusts at all" (it rusts very slightly); original site (Udayagiri likely, not certain).
