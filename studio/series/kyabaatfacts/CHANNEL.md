# Mixed Facts Channel: plan (working notes)

Status: idea stage. Channel name not decided yet (see "Naming" below).
Language: Hindi/Hinglish narration, English on screen (titles, cards, subtitles).
Studio: built on storyreel-studio (painted animated reels 60-120 s + 10-12 min long videos).

## Decisions so far
- Mixed facts channel with 9 categories (all approved by owner).
- Catchy Hinglish hooks: question on screen in the first second, answer revealed in steps.
- Every fact verified against a real source before it becomes a reel (disputed claims framed as "kya sach mein?").
- One weekly signature show: Sach Ya Myth? (Friday).

## Categories and topic bank

### 1. Itihaas Ke Raaz (history)
- Taj Mahal: 22 saal, 20,000 mazdoor - the real cost
- Nalanda: kya library sach mein 3 mahine jali? (disputed - frame as question)
- Akbar padh-likh nahi sakte the, phir bhi itna bada empire kaise chalaya?
- Mohenjo-daro mein 4,500 saal pehle drainage system tha!
- Lothal: duniya ka sabse purana dockyard Gujarat mein
- Sher Shah Suri: the man who gave us the word 'Rupiya'
- Chanakya vs Nanda: one insult that changed history (legend - say so)
- Bajirao: the general who, it's said, never lost a battle (claim - say so)

### 2. Science Wala Sach (everyday science)
- Bijli ka taapmaan suraj ki surface se 5 guna zyada!
- Hichki kyun aati hai?
- Rongte kyun khade hote hain? (goosebumps)
- Ubaasi kyun aati hai? Science ko bhi poora pata nahi!
- Shahad kabhi kharab kyun nahi hota? 3,000 saal purana shahad bhi kha sakte ho
- Kela ek berry hai, strawberry nahi!
- Bachche 300 haddiyon ke saath paida hote hain, bade hokar 206 kyun?

### 3. Desh Ke Ajoobe (wonders of India)
- Kailasa Mandir: ek hi pathar, upar se neeche kaata gaya
- Chand Baori: 13 manzil neeche, 3,500 seedhiyan
- Delhi ka loha stambh 1,600 saal se jang kyun nahi khaata?
- Howrah Bridge: ek bhi nut-bolt nahi!
- Ladakh ki Magnetic Hill: gaadi upar kyun chadhti hai?
- Lonar Jheel: meteor ne banayi jheel
- Meghalaya ke zinda pul - roots se bane bridge
- Mattur: the village where people still speak Sanskrit

### 4. Shabdon Ki Kahani (word and custom origins)
- Shampoo, Jungle, Pyjama, Thug - English ne Hindi se churaye shabd
- 'Rupee' ka matlab kya hai? (Sanskrit 'rupya' = chandi)
- Namaste ke peeche ka reason
- Snakes & Ladders aur Ludo - dono India ke khel!
- Shatranj (chess) ka janm India mein hua - Chaturanga
- Why do Indians say 'cousin brother'?

### 5. Jaanwaron Ki Duniya (animals)
- Octopus ke 3 dil aur neela khoon!
- Kauwe tumhara chehra yaad rakhte hain - and hold grudges
- Sharks are older than trees
- Flamingo gulabi kyun hote hain? Khaane ki wajah se!
- King Cobra: the only snake that builds a nest
- Sher ki dhaariyan sirf baalon pe nahi, skin pe bhi hoti hain

### 6. Antriksh (space)
- Shukra (Venus) ka ek din uske ek saal se lamba!
- Suraj ki roshni humein 8 minute purani milti hai
- Space station pe din mein 16 baar suraj ugta hai
- Chand pe footprints laakhon saal tak rahenge - kyun?
- Chandrayaan-3: India first at the Moon's south pole
- Aryabhata ne 1,500 saal pehle bataya ki dharti ghoomti hai

### 7. Khaane Ka Itihaas (food history)
- Aloo, tamatar, mirch - Indian nahi hain! Portuguese laaye
- Samosa India ka nahi, Central Asia se aaya
- Chai India mein popular kaise hui? The British ad campaign
- Jalebi ka safar: Persia se Bharat
- Biryani ki asli kahani

### 8. Paise Ki Kahani (money)
- ATM PIN sirf 4 digit ka kyun? Inventor ki wife ki wajah se!
- ₹ symbol kisne banaya? A student's design in 2010
- Duniya ka pehla paper money - China
- Notes kaagaz ke nahi, cotton ke bane hote hain

### 9. Sach Ya Myth? (weekly myth-busting show)
- Brihadeeswarar Mandir ki parchhai zameen pe nahi padti? - Myth
- Konark mein chumbak tha?
- Hum dimaag ka sirf 10% use karte hain? - Myth
- Bijli ek jagah do baar nahi girti? - Myth

## Pilot: lessons from instagram-agent-skill (MIT, github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill) — 24 Nov 2026
Applied to R007 + R009 as v2 (opt-in per reel with `KB_SAFE` in shots.js); roll out to all reels if the owner approves.
- **Claim-first hook:** no "अरे, एक बात बताऊँ?" opener; the claim is the first line (their hook scorer: opener versions 15-28/100, claim-first 51-84).
- **Safe zone:** nothing above y 230, below y 1440 or in the right 230 px (Instagram/YouTube UI). Logo + badge share one row at y 236,
  text within x 220-860, subtitles end at y 1400 (`SUB_STYLE`).
- **Loop ending:** the last story line echoes a word from the hook (R007 "ख़राब", R009 "birthday").
- **Captions:** first line ≤125 chars, no em dashes, one clear comment ask.
- Tools used read-only from a scratch copy (hookscore.py, beats.py, caption.py); English-only heuristics, used on the English lines.
